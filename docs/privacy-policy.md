# MuteBar Privacy Policy

MuteBar (the Chrome extension and the companion macOS app) collects no user data.

- The extension has no network access to third parties: no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, no cookies.
- All communication is local to your machine — between the Google Meet tab, the extension's service worker, and the MuteBar macOS app via Chrome's native messaging API and a local Unix socket.
- The only information exchanged is the mute state of your current Google Meet call (a boolean), relayed between the Meet tab and the local app. It never leaves your device.
- No browsing history, personal communications, authentication information, or any other user data is accessed, stored, or transmitted.

MuteBar is open source (MIT); you can audit all of this at https://github.com/sfdye/mute-bar.
